# Privacy policy: RadioFilm Scanner (also called DocScanner)

**Effective date:** 28 September 2026
**Developer:** Insula Neurosciences
**Contact:** insulaneurosciences@gmail.com

RadioFilm Scanner is a document and film scanning app for Android. It is designed so that your scans never leave your phone unless you choose to send them.

## Summary

- The app **does not collect, store on any server, sell or share** any personal data.
- The app has **no internet permission**. It cannot connect to the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- There is **no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking**.

## What the app accesses, and why

**Camera.** Used only while you are scanning, to take the photos you choose to capture. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.

**Photos you import.** When you pick images from your gallery or share them into the app, the app reads only those images.

**Storage (Android 9 and older only).** Used to save your finished scans to your Pictures and Download folders. Newer Android versions do not need this permission.

## Where your data is kept

- **Scans in progress and saved scans** are kept in the app's private storage on your phone, so you can edit them again later. Other apps cannot read this storage.
- **Files you save** (JPEG, PNG or PDF) go to `Pictures/RadioFilm Scanner` and `Download/RadioFilm Scanner` (or the `DocScanner` folders, if you use that name). These are ordinary files on your phone, visible in your Gallery and Files apps.
- **Settings** are kept on your phone. If you use *Export settings*, a settings file is saved wherever you choose.

## Sharing

When you tap **Share**, Android's share menu opens and you choose where the file goes (for example a messaging or email app). The file is handed to that app directly; RadioFilm Scanner has no part in how that app handles it. Please follow your institution's rules when sharing patient images, and use the app's **Anonymise** tool to hide names, IDs and dates first.

## Links

Links in the app (source code, updates, this policy) open in your web browser. The app itself does not visit them.

## Deleting your data

Delete individual scans in **Saved scans**, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings. Files you saved to Pictures or Download stay until you delete them there.

## Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

## Medical disclaimer

RadioFilm Scanner is a scanning and sharing tool, not a medical device. Make diagnostic decisions from the original films or digital images.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. The full history is available in this repository.
